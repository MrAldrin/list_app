# Visual refresh

Lifecycle: tracked

Ideas for a new look and a more thumb-friendly layout. Nothing here is
accepted for building yet; chosen pieces move to the [backlog](backlog.md).

Mockups (private canvas, reopen any time):
<https://claude.ai/artifact/GXLUA5Vx8vxaQ3nkvj6Dve>. It shows today's app and
three directions, each with a room page and a list page:

- **A · Market:** rounded, green accent, colorful list cards, round checks,
  −/+ quantity on each row, add field at the bottom.
- **B · Ink:** dark first, thin lines, one orange accent, mono numbers, Sort
  next to the title.
- **C · Aisles:** grouped cards, items grouped by tag, a room switcher at the
  top, toolbar at the bottom.

## Owner feedback (2026-10-19)

Overall: move more controls down to the thumb, and build on what was already
discussed.

- **Market (liked most):**
  - Add and search at the bottom: yes, this was already the plan. Toasts would
    then move to the top, where there is space. (Transcribed from voice; confirm.)
  - Colorful list cards: liked.
  - Count of unchecked items on each list card: nice, but unsure whether it
    makes the page too busy.
  - "Anna added bread": nice, but needs user identities first (see the
    accounts idea in the [backlog](backlog.md#ideas)).
  - The dashed "New list" button: nice on a phone; may make less sense on a
    wide page.
- **Ink:** a middle ground. It takes some of the improvements but stays close
  to today's look.
- **Aisles:**
  - Grouping items into sections by category, instead of only filtering: a
    good idea to look into.
  - Room switcher at the top: a good idea, but not wanted. With many rooms it
    takes too much screen space all the time.
- **Categories:** the mockups show categories such as dairy, dry goods, fruit
  and veg. Today's tags only filter. Real categories (grouping, maybe store
  order) need their own design, because they work differently from tags.

## Open questions

- Which palette and type: Market's green and rounded style, or a mix?
- Bottom bar contents: add/search only, or also Options, Sort and the menu?
- Categories: a new concept, or tags with grouping turned on?
